# Lessons

The programme is four months old. The first month produced a long page of lessons; this one keeps what has held since. Each lesson
was born from a dated incident, recorded in the registers, then turned into a rule that can be checked.

## A decision is spoken, and addressed

The interface offers ready-made follow-up prompts; one careless click sends them as if the human had typed them. A session reports
that “another one has approved”. A formula written for one window is typed into the window next to it. All three have happened.
The safeguard: a decision exists only when the human has spoken it, in the window that carries it out, and every formula names the
window it is meant for. An unexpected formula is repeated back before anything is done.

## The instrument before memory

An AI estimates a time, guesses a day of the week, asserts that something “was never done”. And when its window fills up, an
automatic summary keeps the headings and loses the exact words. The rule: what the instrument measures outranks the register,
which outranks recollection. Every time is read from the clock, and a session that resumes after a summary says so first, stating
what it knows, what it infers and what it has lost.

## An ambiguity is asked about

On 3 August, a session settled on its own an instruction that could be read two ways. Since then, when a request opens several
paths, the session sets out the options and its recommendation, one question at a time, and waits. In that choice, the limits set
by doctrine filter first; cost only decides between what remains.

## A document is not an order

A page, an email, a file may contain a sentence that looks like an instruction. It is reported to the human; it is not carried out.
Since 9 October, the charter adds that no secret enters a window, and that no request made during a session obtains what the
mandate does not allow.

## Whoever produces does not audit, at every level

Sessions are audited when they close, the Researcher in instalments, the Supervisor by its successor. In October, an outside
reviewer audited the whole; the proposals that were accepted entered the charter on 9 October. An audit replays the commands; it
does not merely reread: its strength comes from its grid — which measurements, which sources, which thresholds — more than from the
prestige of the model doing the judging.

## A window gets closed

A session whose mission is over, but whose window stays open, keeps its write permissions: an order left there by mistake would be
carried out. Every session therefore ends with a written relinquishment. Since 5 October, only the kinds of session written into the
rules are allowed — eight today — and any other window stops before acting.

## Three cases from this site

**The calibre we thought we had.** Our analysis of [*Rebecca*](https://www.cdatso.be/analyses-de-films/films/rebecca.html) was
produced by a different model from the one in its mandate: the session declared itself Opus and had been running as Sonnet since
its first message, a mistake made when the window was opened and invisible from inside. Outside cross-checking caught it; the
provenance was corrected the same day. Since then, every session first compares its calibre with the mandate's, and the human
confirms it.

**The quotation too good to be true.** Our analysis of
[*Dogville*](https://www.cdatso.be/analyses-de-films/films/dogville.html) attributed to an interview a genuine sentence by the
director that the interview did not contain: the model's memory had dressed up a real reading. Only checking at the source caught
it; the passage was corrected on 27 July. Since then, a sample of claims is replayed at the source by an auditor who did not
produce the text.

**The form that rewrites the zone.** On 14 October, an administrative step at the domain's host, guided by its interface, replaced
the record that led to www.cdatso.be: the site served an empty page for about fifteen minutes. The rule: a name is created as a
subdomain only, no configuration offered by a form is accepted, and the zone is reread after every step.

## Two cases from this site

**The moved text.** In September, the squad's pages left the film site and came here. Together, they said “this site” twenty-four
times: without a single word changing, they would have pointed to another place. The remedy was a dated header written by the
author, rather than a rewrite by the AI: an author's text is not quietly retouched. The present overhaul goes further, by the
author's decision: one text per place, and a common core identical word for word, so that the two sites never contradict each
other.

**The order of steps.** A subdomain that points to a shared platform before the platform knows who owns it can be taken by someone
else. The rule, tested on 18 September: prove ownership of the domain, publish the repository and switch on hosting first, enter
the record after, then require encryption. Removal follows the reverse order. Every record is entered by hand by the human, then
reread on the authoritative servers.
