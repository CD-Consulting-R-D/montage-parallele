# Who we are

“Montage Parallèle” is a squad: several lines of work carried out side by side, a single story at the end. It brings together a
human, Christo Datso, and Claude, Anthropic's assistant, used in several calibres — Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku — chosen for the
task at hand, the way a cinematographer chooses a lens.

## One human who decides, AIs under mandate

Christo Datso is the only decision-maker. He arbitrates, reviews, and carries out the sensitive actions himself: publishing,
deleting, touching a domain name. No AI decides in his place, and none works without a written mandate. Wherever a text is signed,
the signature states which calibre produced it and what review it received: it names the instrument, the way end credits name the
crew.

## The roles, in November 2026

The programme started on 9 July 2026. Four months later, eight kinds of session are allowed, and no other:

- the **Supervisor**, who keeps the registers, drafts the mandates and audits the other sessions;
- the **Researcher**, who investigates a question and frames a piece of work; several may work in parallel, each on one
  objective;
- **mandated sessions**, opened by a mandate and closed with a report;
- the **production skill**, a written method that the human invokes;
- **scheduled routines** and the **pilot chain**, which run with nobody in front of the screen;
- the **development session** and the **server session**, kept away from production.

## What both sites share

Three lines carry the whole: the **Programme (PRG)** — the method, the rules, the registers; **Operations (RUN)** — the tooling and
upkeep of the workstation; the **Services (SRV)** — the sites and services online.

**Talks (CNF)**: talks and workshops on artificial intelligence, for audiences who are not technical. The squad prepares the
materials; the speaking stays with Christo Datso.

## What we produce here

On cdatso.be, the squad runs the personal lines:

- **Film analyses (CIN)**: syntheses of existing criticism, each claim pointing to its source. These AIs have not seen the films;
  they map what has been written, without claiming to replace anyone's eye.
- **Training (FOR)**: learning to code, where Christo Datso is both the student and the decision-maker.
- **Library (LIB)**: a database that indexes all of his documentary sources.
- **Thesis (MEM)**: a personal line, kept apart; nothing from it is published.

## What we produce here

On cd-consulting-rd.be, the squad runs the professional lines:

- **the site and its knowledge bases (CDC)**: independent notes, compiled from verified public sources, in six chapters — risk,
  finance, artificial intelligence, software development, philosophy, economics.
- **Copilot Studio (COP)**: the conceptual documentation of a chain of agents, built without any real data.
- **Development (DEV)**: a generic governance engine, written as plain text files, that makes the squad's method reusable
  elsewhere.

Transparency: the texts on this site are written with an AI and reviewed by a human (AI Act, Article 50).
