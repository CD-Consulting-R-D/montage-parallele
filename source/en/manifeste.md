# Manifesto

In this squad, artificial intelligence is not an artificial colleague: it is a set of specialised capabilities, put to work for a
team that moves in short cycles. The aim is not to replace human judgement, but to extend what the team is able to produce, check
and learn. Authority, responsibility and decisions remain human.

Here, these capabilities serve personal work: reading cinema, learning, ordering one's sources.

Here, these capabilities serve professional work: documenting, verifying, making a method transferable.

## Our principles

1. **The human remains responsible.** Every decision that commits a site, its content or its readers is taken by an identified
   person.
2. **The AI acts under mandate.** Each session receives a written role, deliverables and limits; it does not step outside them.
3. **The AI takes no initiative.** What would be useful outside the mandate is proposed; it is not done.
4. **A decision is spoken.** It exists only if the human has said it, with an explicit formula, in the right window.
5. **Memory lives in the registers.** Whatever commits the team is written down at the moment it happens, in a versioned document.
6. **Whoever produces does not audit.** Another session checks, on the evidence; it never decides in the human's place.
7. **Mistakes are kept.** A register is amended by a dated erratum; it is never silently rewritten.
8. **What is published says how it was made.** Each page names its sources, the tool that wrote it and the review it received.
9. **Human skills are cultivated.** The team learns the risks of AI as much as its uses: whatever it hands over to a machine, it
   must still know how to check.

## Our conviction

The future is not an autonomous artificial colleague. It lies in the explicit orchestration of specialised capabilities, under
mandate and under human responsibility, in the service of a stronger collective intelligence. How it works day to day: the
[method](methode.html). What four months have taught us: the [lessons](lecons.html).
